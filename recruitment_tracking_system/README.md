# Recruitment Tracking System

## 1. Project Overview

The Recruitment Tracking System is a database application designed to help a company manage its hiring process.

The system stores information about job postings, applicants, job applications, and interviews.

It helps HR staff organize recruitment activities and track applicants throughout the hiring process, from application submission to the final hiring decision.

## 2. Main Entities

•	Job Posting: Stores information about available positions, including job titles, departments, job descriptions, and posting dates.

•	Applicant: Stores information about job applicants, including their names, email addresses, and phone numbers.

•	Application: Records which applicant applies for which job. It also stores the application date and status, such as Applied, Interview, Accepted, or Rejected.

•	Interview: Stores interview information, including interview dates, times, and results.

## 3. Business Processes

The database will support the following business processes:

•	Create a Job Posting: HR staff can add new job openings and enter information about each position.

•	Register an Applicant: HR staff can record an applicant's contact information.

•	Record a Job Application: HR staff can record which position an applicant applies for and when the application is submitted.

•	Schedule an Interview: HR staff can schedule interviews and record the interview dates and times.

•	Update Application Status: HR staff can update an applicant's status as the hiring process moves forward.

•	Review Recruitment Information: HR staff can review applications, interview schedules, and results to help make hiring decisions.

## 4. Expected Benefits

This database will help HR staff organize recruitment information and track applicants easily throughout the hiring process.
